YOUR PLANT DIED. HIT NEW PLANT.

RUN plays one hour per tick: soil dries over the species' watering interval (7 days for a monstera, 14 for a cactus or snake plant), and a happy plant earns 5 XP a tick. Move a slider to send a fresh reading.
