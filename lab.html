Two things you can play with: the beam trade behind the solar sail frame, and a pocket version of Plantogachi running the app's real game loop.

01 · SOLAR SAIL BEAM TRADE

Each frame edge is a 0.5 m CFRP tube. It has to clear a flexural-rigidity floor of EI ≥ 3.8 N·m², and every gram costs sail thrust. I swept seven diameter and wall pairs and picked 8.0 × 0.15 mm. Run the same trade.

Thin-walled tube, E = 230 GPa, ρ = 1600 kg/m³: the laminate values that reproduce the design point. Stiffness grows with diameter to the fourth power and mass only with the square, so a wide, thin tube wins until the wall gets too thin to roll-wrap or starts to buckle locally. Full case study →
