ABOUT ME

I reason from physics and cost, then validate the design against what I measure. I study mechanical engineering and computer science at Duke, and I build physical systems end to end: structures, enclosures, power, and the firmware that runs them. I also operate what I build, with 30+ flight tests on lab hexacopters and 26 FPV drones built and tuned. This portfolio collects six projects. Five carried from CAD to prototype to test and were revised against measured data; the solar sail is analyzed and specified, with its tests planned for the build phase.

B.S. Mechanical Engineering · A.B. Computer Science · Duke University, Pratt School of Engineering · Expected May 2028 · GPA 3.56

WHERE I'VE BEEN: TEST AND V&V

Most of my paid engineering has been test and validation. I integrated and checked out 15 fixed-wing UAVs and root-caused 30+ Li-ion test failures into QA fixes at Jordan Int'l Air Cargo & Defense, wrote the modal-correlation and 8-cycle thermal-vacuum plans for the solar sail, ran 30+ test flights on three Aurelia X-600 hexacopters and recalibrated their IMU and motion capture from flight logs in QGroundControl, and built and tuned 26 FPV drones. That work taught me what breaks and why: which weak spot-weld raises a battery pack's resistance, which wall sinks in the mold, which cached read goes stale under DMA.

WHERE I'M GOING: DESIGN, MANUFACTURING, EMBEDDED

I want to move that failure judgment upstream into the design itself. I have already done it on my own work. Design: the CFRP solar-sail frame, analyzed and specified in SolidWorks and FEA, the Plantogachi enclosure, and the McRocket toy with its drawing package. Manufacturing: injection-molding DFM, worst-case tolerance stack-up, seal and part-count consolidation, and BOM-and-cost ownership down to $0.36 a unit. Embedded hardware and software: ESP32-S3 and STM32 firmware, TLS telemetry, a drone power-distribution board in Altium, and radar, lidar, and depth cameras time-synced over ROS 2 in the Cyber-Physical Systems Lab. I'm looking for an internship where I own a device from CAD and firmware through the test that proves it. I stay open to any role, though: a product works as one interconnected system, and I learn fastest by seeing how each part, mechanical, electrical, and software, operates against the others.

Independent Project, Supersonic Flow Visualization (Sep 2026 – Present): researching computational models of supersonic flow with Dr. Bavand Keshavarz, ahead of schlieren imaging in a supersonic wind tunnel. Autonomous Swarm Research with Repass Rodgers.
